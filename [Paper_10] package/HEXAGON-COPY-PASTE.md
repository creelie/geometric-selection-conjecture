Title: Explicit String Vacua and the Geometric Selection Conjecture

Abstract:
We combine explicit spectral calculations for the Dwork and weighted Fermat pencils with an audit of the Geometric Selection Conjecture. The residue calculation determines the character labels, cubic couplings and invariant-direction mass maps, while an index argument excludes three standard-embedding families on free quotients of thirteen hypergeometric complete-intersection types. For a nonstandard construction we fix one tetraquadric equation, prove smoothness and a free degree-four action, and calculate equivariant bundle cohomology by an exact determinant certificate. Its projected charged spectrum contains three families and one Higgs doublet pair. At the specified split-bundle point, however, an additional Abelian gauge factor survives, the perturbative Yukawa matrices fail the required rank test, and the tree-level volume remains flat. We give selection and perturbation criteria and a targeted extension construction, keeping their hypotheses separate from verified vacuum data. No vacuum satisfying all nine conditions is constructed; the full Geometric Selection Conjecture remains unproved.
